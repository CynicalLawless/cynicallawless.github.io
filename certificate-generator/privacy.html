Privacy policy

Certificate Generator · Last updated 26 November 2026

Certificate Generator is a Windows desktop app. Everything you make with it, including certificates, templates, names and the record of issued certificates, stays on your PC. The app has no server of its own and sends nothing to its developer or anyone else.

Google Drive backups (optional)

Only if you connect Google Drive in the app's Settings:

  • What the app can reach. It asks Google only for the drive.file permission, which lets it see and change only the files it created in your Drive. It cannot see any of your other files.
  • What it uploads. Copies of its own backups, locked with a password you choose before they leave your PC. Google cannot open them, and neither can anyone without that password. Each file carries the backup's date and the name of the PC that made it, so the app can list them.
  • What it reads about your account. Your email address, name and profile picture, to show which account is connected, and how much Drive space is used and free.
  • Where your sign-in is kept. On your PC only, protected by Windows for your Windows account. It is never put in a backup and never sent anywhere except to Google.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Disconnecting

Disconnect in the app's Settings makes that PC forget your sign-in. Your backups stay in your Drive until you delete them. To remove the app's access from your Google account on every PC, go to your Google Account → third-party connections and remove Certificate Generator.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, this page will say so and show a new date. Questions: open an issue.
