Certificate Generator

A Windows desktop app for certificates of appreciation, completion, attendance and more. It lays names and details onto certificate artwork, one at a time or in batches, then previews, prints and exports them to PDF. It keeps a record of every certificate it issues, so any of them can be made again exactly.

Backups to Google Drive

If you choose to, the app can copy its backups to your own Google Drive. You sign in with Google from the app's Settings, and you can disconnect at any time. The app can see only the files it put in your Drive itself.
